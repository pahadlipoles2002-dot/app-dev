# Fast and Furious Sypnosis
*The Fast and the Furious franchise's first film, released in 2001, is about an LAPD officer who goes undercover to bust a street racing crew suspected of hijacking trucks. The officer, Brian O'Conner, infiltrates the world of Dominic Toretto, the charismatic leader of a rival street racing gang, and finds himself torn between his duty and his growing friendship with Dom. Later films expand on this central relationship and the group's life as they engage in increasingly elaborate heists and confront global threats, such as a drug lord in Fast & Furious (2009) and a cyber-terrorist in later installments*
